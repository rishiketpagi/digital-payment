Assignment 9 
 
1. What barriers must a designer or entrepreneur overcome to turn an idea into action? 
 
a. Lack of confidence in their skills 
b. Fear of negative feedback 
c. Difficulty in securing funding 
d. All of the above 
 
Answer: All of the above 
 
 
2. What is the most important relationship for a startup to manage? 
 
a. Funding sources  
b. Target customers  
c. Technological infrastructure  
d. Market needs 
 
Answer: Target customers 
 
 
3. What types of funding are typically sought by early-stage entrepreneurs or startups? 
 
a. Government grants 
b. Seed funding and crowdfunding 
c. Long-term loans with high interest rates 
d. Equity funds 
 
Answer: Government grants 
                          Seed funding and crowdfunding 
 
 
4. What are some reasons why certain regions may lag in techno-entrepreneurial 
ventures? 
 
a. Education is focused solely on securing jobs 
b. Abundance of natural resources reduces the need for innovation 
c. Government policies discourage entrepreneurship 
d. All of the above 
 
Answer: All of the above 
 
 
5. What is the purpose of the incubation stage in the entrepreneurial journey? 
 
a. Developing a business plan and securing resources 
b. Taking a product idea to commercialization 
c. Conducting advanced research and development 
d. Scaling the business for global markets 
 
Answer: Taking a product idea to commercialization 
 
 
6. How does the role of an entrepreneur differ from that of an innovator? 
 
a. Entrepreneurs focus on maximizing profits, while innovators solve problems without 
business goals 
b. Entrepreneurs see problems as business opportunities, while innovators focus solely on 
solving problems 
c. Entrepreneurs work alone, while innovators always work in teams 
d. Entrepreneurs need formal education, while innovators rely on creativity 
 
Answer: Entrepreneurs see problems as business opportunities, while innovators focus 
solely on solving problems 
 
 
7. What is the purpose of clinical immersion in the healthcare sector for entrepreneurs? 
 
a. To provide administrative training and management skills 
b. To identify unmet needs and gather insights for innovation 
c. To develop marketing strategies for healthcare products 
d. To focus on financial analysis and budget planning 
 
Answer: To identify unmet needs and gather insights for innovation 
 
 
8. What key factor ensures better execution of an entrepreneurial idea? 
 
a. Clear definition of customer needs and market gaps 
b. Strong advertising strategy and brand presence 
c. Advanced office infrastructure and equipment 
d. Rigid adherence to the initial business plan 
 
Answer: Clear definition of customer needs and market gaps 
 
 
 
9. What is the process of transferring an idea to industry through intellectual property or 
technology transfer called? 
 
a. Entrepreneurship 
b. Commercialization 
c. Enterprise development 
d. Incubation 
 
Answer: Commercialization 
 
 
10. Which stage of the entrepreneurial journey focuses on the initial creation and 
development of a startup idea? 
 
a. Idea Generation Stage  
b. Research Stage  
c. Incubation Stage  
d. Scaling Stage 
 
Answer: Idea Generation Stage
